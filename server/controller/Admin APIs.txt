1. KPI & Analytics

GET /api/v1/admin/dashboard/metrics — Aggregate platform Gross Merchandise Value (GMV), total tickets sold, active cinemas, and active movies.

GET /api/v1/admin/dashboard/revenue-analytics — Daily/monthly time-series transaction totals and 16% platform convenience fee cut.

GET /api/v1/admin/dashboard/top-performing — Ranked rankings of top grossing movies, theaters, and operational cities.

2. User Governance
GET /api/v1/admin/users — Paginated list of users with filtering by role (customer, theatre-admin, admin).

PATCH /api/v1/admin/users/:userId/role — Update a user's role (e.g., promote a customer to theatre-admin).

PATCH /api/v1/admin/users/:userId/status — Suspend or activate a user account (isBlocked / isActive).

3. Movie Catalog
PATCH /api/v1/admin/movies/:id/toggle-status — Dedicated fast toggle to enable/disable a movie's isActive flag.

Cloudinary file upload pipeline attached to POST /api/v1/admin/movies and PUT /api/v1/admin/movies/:id (handling multipart poster images).

4. Settlement & Platform Finance
GET /api/v1/admin/finance/transactions — Master audit log of payment records across all theaters.

GET /api/v1/admin/finance/settlements — Theater settlement balance ledger (gross ticket revenue minus platform commission).

POST /api/v1/admin/finance/settlements/:theaterId/mark-paid — Record payout disbursement to cinema partners.

5. Promotions
POST /api/v1/admin/coupons — Create discount coupon codes with minimum spend, discount type, and expiry constraints.
